At first cursor was a really good product and I enjoyed using it.

Now after Elon musk bought it, the pricing made no sense, I can’t use other models than Grok, the advance of other like Claude code, Mistral Vibe, Codex etc…

In like 1 years, the pricing changed at least 3 times, now I have a unified account with Grok and X but the experience in the IDE is worse because I can use only Grok model now.

The things which was the last stroke is :

THERE IS LITTERALLY NO BUTTON TO CANCEL YOUR SUBSCRIPTION !!!

In th UI of cursor admin, you just can upgrade the pricing, pay more for less what a deal lol.

So I used <BaseLink text="oleg-koval/cursorport" url="https://github.com/oleg-koval/cursorport" /> and make the switch again :D.

My setup now is Mistral Vibe with Mistral large 4 or GLM and VS Code with no AI feature enabled.

PS: I use sometimes Zed and now a lot Vim since I work mainly in infra so I will be changing again one day.
