<WebLinkSection emoji="🦜" url="https://github.com/drumih/turbo-fieldfare" text="Turbo fieldflare" templateClass="mt-4">
**Gemma 4 26B-A4B inference in about 2 GB of RAM**
A custom Swift + Metal runtime for any Apple Silicon Mac, even the 8 GB ones.
</WebLinkSection>

<WebLinkSection emoji="😯" url="https://bunqueue.dev/" text="Bunqueue" templateClass="mt-4">
Bunqueue is **zero-infrastructure background work**: a job queue with priorities, retries, cron and a dead letter queue, a <BaseLink text="durable workflow engine" url="https://bunqueue.dev/guide/workflow/" /> with saga rollback and human approval gates, and a native <BaseLink text="MCP server" url="https://bunqueue.dev/guide/mcp/" /> for AI agents. One process, one SQLite file. No Redis, no broker, nothing to operate. The queue speaks to <BaseLink text="six languages" url="https://bunqueue.dev/guide/sdks/" />; the workflow engine runs in-process on Bun.
</WebLinkSection>
